# Community expectations

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss the work without harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks. Do not publish another person's private information, diagnostics or credentials.

Luke Ivers may remove inappropriate content or limit participation. Moderation is best effort; this project has no dedicated response team or confidential conduct-reporting service. For abusive content on GitHub, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Avoid publishing sensitive allegations in project issues. Security and privacy vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting route](SECURITY.md).
